I am an MSc student in Data Science at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/) and a Graduate Student Researcher at [VILAB](https://vilab.epfl.ch/). My research centers on **multimodal foundation models** and **representation learning**. While large language models are powerful, modeling vision purely through language-centric approaches feels counter-intuitive. I believe multimodal intelligence requires efficient representations that genuinely leverage rich perceptual signals. I am interested in how foundation models can learn shared representations across modalities to reason and generate in a unified way, making them more adaptable, reliable, and useful in open-ended real-world settings.

I earned a BSc in Artificial Intelligence from the [Polytechnic University of Catalonia (UPC)](https://www.upc.edu/). Previously, I completed my bachelor thesis as a Research Intern at [CVC-UAB](https://www.cvc.uab.es/), under the supervision of [Joost van de Weijer](https://www.cvc.uab.es/people/joost-van-de-weijer/).

Outside of research, I enjoy working out, hiking, and drawing.
