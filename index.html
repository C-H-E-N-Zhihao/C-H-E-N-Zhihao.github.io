Zhihao Chen

MSc Data Science student at EPFL · Graduate Student Researcher at VILAB

Lausanne, Switzerland

zhihao.chen@epfl.ch

I am an MSc student in Data Science at EPFL and a Graduate Student Researcher at VILAB, advised by Prof. Amir Zamir. I work on multimodal representation learning: how models can learn disentangled, generalizable representations across modalities, so that features can be reused zero-shot and new cross-modal capabilities emerge.

My view is that vision should not be treated as an add-on to language. Multimodal intelligence needs representations that take rich perceptual signals seriously, and I am interested in foundation models that share such representations to understand and generate in a unified way.

Before EPFL, I earned a BSc in Artificial Intelligence from UPC in Barcelona. There I studied cross-modal catastrophic forgetting in unified multimodal models with Dr. Joost van de Weijer at LAMP, CVC-UAB, worked on flow matching for action segmentation and anticipation with Dr. Mariella Dimiccoli at IRI-UPC, and on context-aware action recognition with Prof. Auke Jan IJspeert at BioRob, EPFL.

Outside of research, I enjoy working out, hiking, and drawing.

I am looking for research internships starting in Summer 2027, in multimodal representation learning and generative modeling. Feel free to reach out.
